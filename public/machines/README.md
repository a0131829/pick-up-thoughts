# Watercolor writing machines

Generated with OpenAI image generation on 2026-10-06 for the approved watercolor concept.

- `typewriter-watercolor.png`: gray-blue front-facing retro typewriter, warm ivory round keys, brass details, watercolor pigment and paper grain, transparent background, no paper sheet (rendered separately).
- `shredder-watercolor.png`: terracotta front-facing retro manual paper shredder, top roller and crank, empty lower paper bin, matching watercolor treatment, transparent background, no paper sheet.

These are the generation design briefs, not verbatim tool prompts. The rendered webpage uses layered watercolor images in Three.js; it is a 2.5D illustration, not a fully modeled machine. Paper and ink share one canvas texture and one feed motion; cut strips retain their original ink UVs. A curved sheet with pulp fibers narrows for the shredder. The typebar follows the editor insertion point. Native textarea retains IME and accessibility; glass controls reuse the site style. The public demo uses a local lake backdrop.
